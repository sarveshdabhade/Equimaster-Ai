EQUIMASTER-AI: CURRENT TECHNICAL REPORT
Release review: 19 November 2026

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

The primary application is a Windows/Python 3.13 Streamlit research dashboard.
It combines market context, technical charts, LSTM forecasts, public company
fundamentals and evidence-linked news. It is not an automated trading engine.
The separate webapp/ prototype and pooled-model experiments are preserved but
are not presented as the active per-ticker forecasting release.

2. APPLICATION AND DATA FLOW

app.py delegates the market view to views/home_view.py. The stock analyzer is
pages/stock_analyzer.py; it loads research sections lazily. Shared components
and assets/app.css define the visual theme. Quotes, price-history dates and
model-training dates are shown separately, because they can have different ages.

config.py defines 58 company tickers, the NIFTY index and horizons [1,5,22,63].
src/update_data.py refreshes provider OHLCV, retains older history outside rolling
requests and removes incomplete current-session bars. src/preprocessor.py builds
indicators, rejects failed processing and retains identical active snapshot bytes.
Data quality is checked separately against recent provider NIFTY session dates.
This reference is not an independent exchange holiday calendar.

3. TRAINING AND MODEL CONTRACT

scripts/train_manager.py is the canonical bulk entry point. It checks active
dataset/model/scaler hashes and trains only missing or changed bundles when
--only-missing is selected. Each ticker needing fitting must first pass the
20-fresh-verified-article gate. News is an operational prerequisite, not an LSTM
input feature. Insufficient coverage preserves the previous active model.

src/training.py owns the fitting path. Models use 60-session windows, two 50-unit
LSTM layers with dropout, a dense layer and a residual correction to the latest
close. Separate direct-output models cover 1,5,22 and 63 sessions. Splits are
chronological; overlapping multi-session targets are purged at boundaries and
the scaler is fitted inside the pre-test history. Early stopping and return
shrinkage use validation data. Residual intervals also use that validation
segment, so independent interval calibration remains future research work.

Each immutable bundle stores its processed.csv, scaler.pkl, metadata.json,
h1/h5/h22/h63.weights.h5 and backtest_h*.csv. Metadata records feature order,
pipeline version, fitting/test dates, package versions, model/data/scaler hashes,
news validation and persistence comparisons. An atomic active pointer publishes
matching artifacts together. A source-data change during fitting prevents the
new bundle from being activated.

4. INFERENCE AND EVALUATION

src/forecast.py resolves the selected bundle, checks the feature contract and
returns a direct price path and empirical residual bounds. Stored backtests
retain prediction origins, target dates, predicted closes and actual closes.
The release evaluator reports terminal-target MAE, RMSE, MAPE, direction sign
agreement and persistence metrics. Terminal interval coverage is computed on
those same target rows; all-lead path coverage is retained as a separate field.
Raw-price MAE is not averaged across differently priced stocks as a single
cross-stock quality score. Frozen-model tests are not refitted walk-forward tests.

The dashboard also shows a heuristic LSTM/news/fundamentals estimate alongside
the LSTM baseline. Its incremental predictive value has not been validated.
The separately trained learned-fusion path stays shadow-only; optional LLM
synthesis does not make the price forecast a validated language-model forecast.

5. NEWS, RETRIEVAL AND OPTIONAL LLM COMPONENTS

News merges independent provider responses, filters recognized publishers and
issuer aliases, preserves source URLs, normalizes timestamps and deduplicates
URL/headline variants. A bundled name registry makes a fresh checkout independent
of local fundamentals caches. Focused publisher and date-window searches recover
stories hidden by broad rankings; they retain the same filters and news threshold.
Undated articles cannot demonstrate freshness for training. Historical retrieval
requires publication and first-observation timestamps no later than its cutoff.

Keyword sentiment and TF-IDF retrieval are the core paths. FinBERT, embeddings and
hosted/local LLM evidence synthesis are optional. Model downloads require explicit
configuration. Citation identifiers and output schema are checked, but citation
traceability is not proof that a claim is semantically grounded or predicts prices.
Independent regional labels, confidence calibration and temporal fusion ablations
remain necessary before stronger claims.

6. FUNDAMENTALS AND VALUATION

The company schema has 98 supported metric slots, not 98 guaranteed observations
per issuer. Sources, reporting periods, accounting basis and monetary currencies
are retained. Current snapshots are archived for future point-in-time studies;
today's financial values are not inserted into historical training dates.

The company page and universe exporter share calculate_fundamental_valuation.
Industrial FCFE requires same-period CFO, capital expenditure and reported net
borrowing. Financial scenarios can use residual income. Compatible industry peers
are preferred, with explicit lower-confidence sector fallback scenarios. Inputs
with unverified currencies or unsupported financial anchors are withheld. Default
discount-rate assumptions are research parameters, not quoted current market rates.
The 252-session excess-return model requires matured labels and validation; its
unavailable state remains explicit. No synthetic BUY/SELL model results are supplied.

7. OPERATIONS AND FAILURE BEHAVIOUR

daily_pipeline.py runs market refresh, preprocessing, training/evaluation,
fundamentals and the return-model readiness check as bounded subprocess stages.
Failures and completed stages are written incrementally. A shared OS lock prevents
overlapping pipeline processes. Stage timeout/cancellation stops its child tree.
Atomic writes use unique temporary files and bounded Windows reader-lock retries;
unchanged CSV snapshots are not needlessly replaced. Failed refresh/fitting keeps
previous valid data and models. Forced process termination can leave a last-running
status until a subsequent run; the Task Scheduler result and active hashes remain
separate evidence of completion.

The primary Windows task uses the project .venv interpreter and a weekday 18:30
Asia/Kolkata trigger. It runs while the registering user is logged in. A legacy
duplicate task remains inaccessible to this process; administrator cleanup is
documented, and the shared lock protects against overlap.

8. INSTALLATION, RELEASE AND REPRODUCIBILITY

Install requirements-lock.txt in a fresh Python 3.13 environment. Restore the
runtime archive or run the daily pipeline, then launch streamlit run app.py.
Optional browser tooling is in requirements-ui.txt. Core tests do not require
model downloads or provider availability. Use scripts/audit_universe.py,
valuation_coverage.py, validate_runtime.py --require-current, evaluate_watchlist.py,
evaluate_cost_sensitivity.py, evaluate_news.py and validate_browser.py for checks.

scripts/package_release.py requires a clean reviewed source tree and current
bundles. Its --ci-report option requires successful remote CI for the exact source
commit. It packages current models, datasets, fundamentals, news archives and
evidence, creates a consistent SQLite backup, records per-file hashes and verifies
ZIP CRCs and required bundle entries. Environments, provider cookies and secrets
are excluded. Existing historical models/data are retained locally.

Terminal coverage uses the target date scored by MAE/MAPE. Path coverage averages
all lead steps and is retained separately. Reports before 17 November used the path
average under the former coverage field. Intervals use validation residuals; that
segment also selects stopping weights and shrinkage, so these are not independently
calibrated or guaranteed conformal intervals. These are frozen fitted-model tests,
not monthly refitted walk-forward results or proof of an untouched final study.
The universe is the currently configured stock set, not a point-in-time constituent
database. Historical comparisons retain survivorship and corporate-action limits.

## News and fundamentals evidence

The keyword scorer was evaluated without fitting on 2,264 expert-labelled English
FinancialPhraseBank sentences: accuracy 76.19%, majority
baseline 61.44%, macro F1
0.626, and negative recall 27.06%.
This is not an Indian-market return benchmark; confidence remains uncalibrated.
The current annotation export contains 4,527 unlabelled
article rows across 58 companies. Independent human labels are still required.

Valuation reports share the company-page calculation, with default research
discount-rate assumptions, reported company beta, verified monetary units and
same-period FCFE inputs. Confidence labels describe method/input coverage; the
ranges are scenarios, not statistical confidence intervals. Page overrides can
produce different scenarios. The 252-session fundamentals return model remains
gated by matured point-in-time labels (minimum 30 matured snapshot months and 300
samples); no historical fundamentals were backfilled using today's observations.
